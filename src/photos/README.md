# Photos

Drop photos here and they appear on the home page, in black and white.
Name them exactly:

- `office.jpg`: the front of a DMV office or its sign
- `papers.jpg`: hands holding forms or a birth certificate application
- `counter.jpg`: someone getting help at a counter or desk
- `phone.jpg`: a phone in someone's hand with First ID open

`.png` and `.webp` work too. Keep each under about 300 KB (1600 px on the long side is plenty).
Only use photos we took ourselves or have the rights to, and no strangers' faces.
